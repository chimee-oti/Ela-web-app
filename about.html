Our mission

To move goods and people across Africa and beyond with a reliability the market has never been able to take for granted. We advance trade, logistics and transport on one accountable system, turning movement that has always been fragmented and uncertain into infrastructure that businesses and travellers can build their operations and their lives around.

Our vision

To become the infrastructure Africa moves on. We are building toward a continent where any business or traveller, in any market, reaches first for ELA to move what matters, and where logistics that is reliable, transparent and accountable is no longer the exception but the standard.
